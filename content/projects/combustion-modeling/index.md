Advanced 3D reactive CFD modeling of turbulence-combustion interactions in
low-carbon fuel heavy-duty engines, using CONVERGE with RNG k-ε turbulence
modeling, validated against experimental data from FPT Industrial. Includes
0D/1D combustion analysis in Cantera.
