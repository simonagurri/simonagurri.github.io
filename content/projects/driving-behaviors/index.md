Large-scale mobility pattern analysis and powertrain optimization. Processed a
200k-vehicle European dataset in Python/pandas to identify urban and
extra-urban driving cycles, with a k-means clustering model grouping vehicles by
usage pattern to inform powertrain choice. Data provided by Stellantis.
