## Expertise in CFD

My work in CFD has been focusing particularly in modeling low-carbon fuel internal combustion engines. I've employed CONVERGE CFD for 3D RANS simulations, integrating advanced models to capture complex turbulence-chemistry interactions. This experience has honed my skills in:

- Advanced CFD modeling
- Turbulence model development
- Boundary condition optimization
- High-performance computing

## Bridging CFD with Transportation Systems

While my core expertise lies in CFD, I've also applied these skills to broader transportation challenges. I've worked on projects ranging from next-generation freight trains to electric yard tractors, always with an eye on improving energy efficiency and sustainability.

## Future Directions: AI and ML in CFD

Looking ahead, I'm excited about the potential of artificial intelligence and machine learning in CFD. I believe these technologies can revolutionize our approach to fluid dynamics simulations, offering new insights and efficiencies. I'm actively seeking opportunities to expand my knowledge in this area and apply AI/ML techniques to CFD problems.

## Beyond the Lab

When I'm not immersed in CFD simulations, you'll find me exploring the outdoors through hiking, running, and mountain biking. I'm also an amateur photographer, capturing urban and natural landscapes that often inspire my scientific work.


**I'm always eager to collaborate on challenging projects that push the boundaries of CFD and its applications in sustainable technologies. If you're interested in discussing potential collaborations or just want to chat about the latest in CFD, feel free to reach out!**
