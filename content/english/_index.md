**Research fellow working on CFD for internal combustion engines** in a project founded by the Italian Ministry of Economics (B4IA - Innovative Autonomous Bus fueled with Hydrogen).


During my **PhD in Energetics** at Politecnico di Torino, I moved between **railway and transport systems and computational fluid dynamics**, and I still like to keep one foot in both worlds.


Now I focus mostly on numerical simulations of engine flows, combustion, and thermal systems, and I enjoy collaborating with people who care about cleaner and smarter transport.


I am currently exploring **postdoctoral opportunities** in fluid dynamics, energy, and sustainable transport in Europe and North America.


Outside the lab, you will probably find me running, on a padel or tennis court, or somewhere in the mountains with a camera in hand.
​

_This website collects my research projects, publications, and collaborations in energy and transport systems._
