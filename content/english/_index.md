**I am a Research Fellow in reactive CFD for internal combustion engines** at Politecnico di Torino, working on low-carbon and alternative fuels (hydrogen, CNG, ethanol, methanol) for heavy-duty applications. My current project, B4IA, is funded by the Italian Ministry of Economics and develops an autonomous bus powered by a hydrogen internal combustion engine.


My work centers on **combustion and turbulence modeling, chemical kinetics, and high-performance simulation**, always validated against experimental engine data. I am currently extending this line toward ammonia combustion.


During my **PhD in Energetics**, alongside engine CFD I also worked on **railway and freight transport systems**, a line I still carry two publications away from closing. That background gives me a system-level view of how engine-scale choices propagate to fleet and network performance, which I find valuable when combustion research meets real transport applications.


I am currently exploring **postdoctoral and R&D opportunities** in combustion, energy, and sustainable transport, in Europe and North America.


Outside the lab, you will probably find me running, on a padel or tennis court, or somewhere in the mountains with a camera in hand.
